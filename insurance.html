Frequently Asked Questions

Insurance Queries

1. What types of insurance do you offer?

We offer a comprehensive range of insurance plans including health insurance, life insurance, auto insurance, and home insurance. Each plan can be customized to suit your specific requirements.

2. How can I get a quote?

You can get a quote by contacting us directly via phone or email. Alternatively, fill out the form on our website and a representative will get back to you with a personalized quote.

3. What is the claim process?

Notify us about the incident as soon as possible, fill out the necessary paperwork, and our team will review your claim for payout based on your policy terms.

4. Can I adjust my coverage?

Yes, you can adjust your coverage at any time. Contact us to discuss your needs and we'll help modify your plan accordingly.

5. What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Mpesa, all major credit cards, bank transfers, and cheques with flexible monthly, quarterly, or annual payment schedules.

6. How can I contact customer service?

Contact us via phone, email, or the contact form on our website. Our team is dedicated to providing the best service and will respond as quickly as possible.
